Background

I recently completed my PhD at Cambridge, researching radiomics and medical imaging in oncology. I now work as a Public Health Officer at the Ministry of Health, Brunei Darussalam.

My background spans Biomedical Engineering (UCL) and Radiation Biology (Oxford). Scientist-engineer by training, but I've always been comfortable working across disciplines.

I'm particularly interested in how data and AI can strengthen public health systems and decision-making. I also make films, animations, and books — science communication has always felt like the other half of the job.
