Background

I recently completed my PhD at Cambridge, researching radiomics and medical imaging in oncology. I now work as a Public Health Officer at the Ministry of Health, Brunei Darussalam.

My background spans Biomedical Engineering (UCL) and Radiation Biology (Oxford). I think rigorously, but I care about whether the output means something to real people.

I'm interested in how data and AI tools translate from research into real health systems. I also make films, animations, and books — science communication has always felt like the other half of the job.
